About

Trip Kit drills the Japanese you meet on the road from Osaka through Shiga and Gifu into the Hida mountains and Toyama: signs, place names and phrases for hosts. A sibling of Kanji Trainer. Everything stays on this device; it works offline after the first visit.

Readings checked against JMdict and KANJIDIC2 (© Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, CC BY-SA 4.0), via kotobako-data and kanji-data. JLPT levels from Jonathan Waller's JLPT lists. Kanji keywords from James Heisig's Remembering the Kanji (via kanji-data). Lesson numbers refer to Minna no Nihongo.

Signs, notes and phrases: written for this trip; check local rules on the spot (garbage days differ per town).
